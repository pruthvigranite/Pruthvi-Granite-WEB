Absolute Black Granite

Description

Create a sleek, bold, and sophisticated statement with Absolute Black Granite. As the name suggests, this stone offers a deep, solid, and highly consistent black canvas without heavy veining or varied patterns. Its uniform appearance makes it incredibly versatile, serving as the perfect high-contrast anchor for bright, modern, or minimalist interiors.

Best For: Modern kitchen countertops, sleek bathroom vanities, and striking floor tiles.
Key Vibe: Elegant, timeless, and dramatic.

Specifications

  • Origin: India
  • Finish: Polished
  • Thickness: 20mm, 30mm

Applications

  • Countertops
  • Flooring
  • Walls
